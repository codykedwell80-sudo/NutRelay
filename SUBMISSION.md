# Nut November submission draft

Project: NutRelay — Blackout Lab
One-liner: A Cashu offline-readiness planner that shows how denomination choices affect the ability to make exact-change payments during an internet outage.
Suggested tracks: The Nuttiest Idea / Best Design Nut / Freshly Minted Nut (if eligible); judges consider all categories.
Project description: NutRelay imports NUT-01 public mint keysets and checks NUT-02 keyset IDs, builds denomination plans from a forecast payment list, and simulates compact versus prepared inventories without contacting a mint. A dashboard visualizes payment coverage, proof counts, fees and randomized outage scenarios. It exports non-secret JSON plans. The festival demo covers 0/10 forecast payments with a minimum-proof compact inventory versus 10/10 with a preallocated inventory; 40 seeded pooled stress trials yield 0.8% versus 78.2% coverage in that particular scenario. These are simulated, not real payments.
